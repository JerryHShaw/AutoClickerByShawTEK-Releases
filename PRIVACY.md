# AutoClicker by ShawTEK — Privacy Notice

*Last updated: November 2026*

AutoClicker by ShawTEK does not collect, sell or share your personal information. It has no
accounts, advertising, analytics or telemetry. This notice explains exactly what the app stores and
the few times it uses the internet. The same notice is shown by the installer and is available from
**Help ▾ → About AutoClicker…** in the app.

## What stays on your computer

- **Recordings and schedules you save:** mouse positions, click types, timings and, if *Record
  keystrokes* is on (it is on by default), everything you type while recording — **including any
  passwords**. These are saved as plain-text files in your AutoClicker folder,
  `%APPDATA%\AutoClickerByShawTEK`. Do not record passwords or other sensitive information.
- **Your settings**, such as your custom hotkey.
- **A list of the action files you used most recently**, which Windows also shows on AutoClicker's
  taskbar jump list.
- **Crash reports**, if the app ever runs into an error, in the `Logs` folder inside your AutoClicker
  folder. They can contain folder paths that include your Windows user name.

None of this is sent anywhere by the app.

## When the app uses the internet

- **Update checks.** When AutoClicker starts, and when you choose *Check for Updates* or *View Update
  History*, it downloads small files from GitHub (github.com), where AutoClicker's releases are
  published. GitHub sees your IP address and the app's version number, as with any download.
  [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies) applies. If you
  install an update, the installer is downloaded from the same place.
- **Donate.** The Donate buttons open PayPal's website in your web browser. AutoClicker never sees or
  handles your payment details. PayPal's privacy statement applies.
- **Contact support.** The support link opens a new email in your own email program. Nothing is sent
  unless you send the email.

## Sharing crash reports

Crash reports are only shared if you choose to email one to support. They are used only to fix the
problem you reported.

## Removing your information

Uninstalling AutoClicker leaves your AutoClicker folder in place so you don't lose your recordings.
To remove everything, delete the folder `%APPDATA%\AutoClickerByShawTEK` after uninstalling.

## Children

AutoClicker does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this notice changes, the new version will be included with the next AutoClicker release and
published here.
